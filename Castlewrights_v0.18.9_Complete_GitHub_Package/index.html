Design questions being tested

Crossbowmen (X) fire from range and sally groups can intercept them. Shieldbearers (H) resist frontal arrows by 40%, but flanking arrows and melee bypass the shield. Check the pre-siege scouting report. Melee defenders now intercept an approaching attacker once per turn, and at most two attackers engage the keep simultaneously. Sallies prioritize crossbowmen, then nearby attackers, and return through any reachable friendly entrance. The keep and gatehouse are mandatory. The primary gate can be interior; the castle must have a passable route through it to the outside. One optional sally port can replace an intact wall edge. Troops may cross it; civilians and courtyard-service connections cannot. Attackers must breach it. New in v0.18.7: retrofit basic wall edges with a wall walk for an inexpensive archer post; river terrain, high ground and a vulnerable supply depot offer different Chapter 2 incentives. A valid castle needs an unobstructed courtyard route from the gate to the keep and at least two connected open courtyard cells. Barracks, granaries, workshops and smithies must touch this network to function; disconnected buildings are gray with an exclamation mark. Use gold for every action, including walls, building upgrades, recruitment and repairs. Basic walls occupy grid edges; 90° corners place two adjoining edges, while 45° diagonal corner walls cut off two sides of a cell (simplified collision). Towers, barracks, granaries, smithies and workshops occupy cells. Surviving workshops grant gold, while granaries retain up to two extra surviving soldiers. Both walls and living infrastructure can be destroyed. Three sieges test one persistent fortress. This is a deliberately simplified new simulation, not a drop-in replacement for v0.16.1.
