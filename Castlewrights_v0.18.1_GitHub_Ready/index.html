Design questions being tested

The keep and gatehouse are mandatory. A valid castle needs an unobstructed courtyard route from the gate to the keep and at least two connected open courtyard cells. Barracks, granaries, workshops and smithies must touch this network to function; disconnected buildings are gray with an exclamation mark. Use gold for every action, including walls, building upgrades, recruitment and repairs. Basic walls occupy grid edges; 90° corners place two adjoining edges, while 45° diagonal corner walls cut off two sides of a cell (simplified collision). Towers, barracks, granaries, smithies and workshops occupy cells. Surviving workshops grant gold, while granaries retain up to two extra surviving soldiers. Both walls and living infrastructure can be destroyed. Three sieges test one persistent fortress. This is a deliberately simplified new simulation, not a drop-in replacement for v0.16.1.
