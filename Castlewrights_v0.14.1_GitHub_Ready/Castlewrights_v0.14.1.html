Experimental rules

Persistence keeps placed structures and battle damage between waves. A short draft offers 3 picks (4×4) or 4 picks (5×5); repairing consumes a pick and demolishing consumes one of two renovation actions. Full rebuild uses 8/11 picks each wave. Barracks supply 2 housing per cell; keep supplies 2. Each intact granary adds 2 retention slots to the baseline 1. Enemies approach from the outer battlefield; only deployed archers fire from ranged posts, and melee defenders react near their assigned courtyard or gate positions. Keep is the only defeat objective. These are provisional experimental rules.
